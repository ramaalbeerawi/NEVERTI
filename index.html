Inspired by Nefertiti

Named after Queen Nefertiti, a timeless symbol of beauty and strength.

Neverti makes color for every face: the first line of liner, the last touch of gloss, and everything in between. Every shade is made to look as good on you as it does in the tube, which is why you can try it on before you buy it.

Good to know

Shipping

Orders are packed within two to four working days. Delivery time and fees will be shown at checkout once payments are connected.

Returns

Unopened products can be returned within 14 days. Opened makeup can't be returned for hygiene reasons.

Questions

Do I need an account to browse?

No. You only sign in when you add something to your bag.

Is my photo uploaded in the try-on?

Never. The photo stays on your device.

How do I choose my foundation shade?

Use Find my shade. It reads your skin from a selfie and suggests the closest shades.
