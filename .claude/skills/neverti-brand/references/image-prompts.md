# Image prompts that work for Neverti

Lessons learned with Meta AI / other generators:
- Short prompts follow colors better. Put the main color at the very start and use only one color family per image.
- Too-close macro of glossy lips looks gross; avoid words like "juicy", "wet", "thick gloss".
- Wide-open eyes look staring/creepy; have the eye looking down so the lid makeup shows.
- To change one thing, edit an existing approved image ("Change only X. Keep everything else exactly the same.") instead of generating from scratch.
- Always generate 3–4 variants and pick the best.

## Shared style (end every section prompt with this)
warm olive skin with visible pores and light freckles, soft warm daylight, warm cream background on the right side, luxury makeup campaign photography, ultra sharp focus, high detail, wide horizontal 16:9

## Approved section images
- Lips: front view, slight tilt, defined rose-brown liner, rose-red matte lipstick, a touch of gloss on the lower lip.
- Eyes: eye looking down, soft matte pink eyeshadow.
- Brows: brushed-up brow with tinted gel, eye closed.
- Face: still needed — flawless even skin after concealer and foundation, only a few faint freckles, soft rosy-peach blush, no redness, no shiny nose.

## Negative prompt base
cartoon, illustration, 3D render, plastic skin, distorted features, extreme angle, side profile

## Model photo for try-on
Front-facing, eyes closed, lips closed, no makeup, pink silk headband, hair pulled back. Edit only from the original bare-face photo, never from an already edited one.
